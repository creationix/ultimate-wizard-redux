How to play

Each chamber fits on one screen. Take the key, then reach the door. Gems are optional - collect them all for a perfect clear.

MoveArrow keys or A / DD-pad
Climb, portalsUp / DownD-pad up / down
JumpSpace or Z - hold for a higher jumpJump button
Cast spellX, Shift or EnterSpell button
Pause / restartEsc / RPause button
  • Ladders and ropes: hold Up to grab on. You will not fall off by accident: jump to hop off, or step sideways onto a floor.
  • Ledges: wooden ledges can be jumped up through. Hold Down to drop through one.
  • Hearts: you have three. A creature, flame or very long fall costs one; pits and spikes cost one and put you back on the last safe ground. Nothing you collected is lost.
  • Cracked stone collapses shortly after you step on it.
  • Flame jets switch on and off in a steady rhythm.
  • Portals: stand inside and tap Up to step out of the matching portal.

Scrolls

You carry one spell at a time; picking up a scroll replaces it.

  • Bolt - three shots that destroy a creature.
  • Frost - freezes every creature for five seconds. Frozen creatures are harmless.
  • Feather - six seconds of gentle falling. No fall can hurt you.
  • Ward - four seconds of protection from creatures and fire.

A gamepad works too. Stuck? Pause and choose Watch solution.
